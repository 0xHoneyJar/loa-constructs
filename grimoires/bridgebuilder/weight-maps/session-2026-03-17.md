## What Happened

the operator arrived with a question: "how does w3ga integrate with our existing systems?"

WEAVER's first instinct was to map the weight, not to plan the integration. the mapping sequence:

1. **w3ga** — heavy analytics, light infrastructure, GA4-welded transport. shape affinity with ruggy's empty domain slot. complementary to beehive's qualitative blindness.

2. **ruggy** — massive inherited infrastructure (47 endpoints), zero domain intelligence. the bear in a suit that's too big. gravity pointed at the wrong thing.

3. **beehive** — deepest qualitative pipeline in the network. 24 skills, Level 3 diagnostic, RLM isolation. blind to what users DO (only knows what they SAY).

4. **the product ecosystem** — 6 repos, all with Umami pageviews, none with error tracking, none with client events (except cubquests' 4 DataFast events), none with agent detection. the universal gaps are the real finding.

## What Emerged

### the weight mapping methodology

WEAVER's core contribution: three levels (surface → motivation → position) and seven weight dimensions (domain, surface, motivation, affinity, attention, gravity, maintenance). the methodology didn't exist before this session. it was designed to answer "where does w3ga sit in our network?" and turned out to be general enough to map anything.

### gravity at two scales

the operator confirmed gravity as the key dimension. app gravity (how much products need something) and construct gravity (how much constructs pull toward each other) operate at different scales. the gravity anomalies — dynamic-auth as island, k-hole as cultural well, ruggy's misdirected pull — were the most interesting findings.

### three forms of composition

the k-hole weight map revealed this. mapping k-hole's position exposed that cultural influence (7 constructs feel k-hole's pull, none are wired to it) is a real form of gravity that topology tools can't measure. this led to naming the three forms:

1. **structural** (dependency edges) — observer↔crucible
2. **linguistic** (shared vocabulary) — artisan's taste tokens
3. **cultural** (gravitational influence) — k-hole's depth philosophy

the insight: **linguistic composition is the most resilient form of integration.** the vocabulary IS the integration. this is how w3ga should connect — through shared event language, not through dependency.

### lenses in the operator OS

modes (FEEL/ARCH/DIG/SHIP) answer "what kind of work." lenses (KEEPER/WEAVER/Gecko) answer "how you're seeing." they're orthogonal. you can be in ARCH mode wearing the WEAVER lens. this distinction didn't exist before and resolves the question of where WEAVER sits relative to the existing cognitive frame.

### ruggy's navigation path

ruggy doesn't need to own w3ga. ruggy needs to navigate toward it through weight. the shape affinity thread (w3ga's agent query architecture → ruggy's empty domain slot) is the template. the 15 ecosystem queries are spec'd. 8 of 15 are available with current data. the highest-leverage first move is agent-vs-human classification on existing install data.

## What Surprised

1. **the methodology emerged from the question, not before it.** WEAVER's weight mapping wasn't designed in advance and then applied. it was designed by being applied. the w3ga question shaped the methodology.

2. **cultural gravity is the dominant force in the network and the invisible one.** k-hole's 7 inbound references with zero outbound declarations is the strongest gravitational pattern in the ecosystem, and the topology graph says it's an island.

3. **the product observability gap is universal and trivial to diagnose.** every product has the same gap (no Sentry, no events, no replay, no structured logging). ruggy's first triage query has a trivial answer. the hard part isn't diagnosis — it's deciding which tool to adopt.

4. **artisan proved that linguistic composition works in practice.** taste tokens compose products with artisan's vocabulary without dependency declarations. the language IS the API. this pattern should be the model for how w3ga's event taxonomy enters the ecosystem.
