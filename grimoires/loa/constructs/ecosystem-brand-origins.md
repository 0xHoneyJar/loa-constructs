## Part I: The Genesis Product — rektdrop-interface

### What It Is

A theatrical financial confession booth. Users connect their Berachain wallet, the system excavates their NFT trading losses across Mibera, Steady Teddy, Bullas, Henlo, and Nichibis collections, assigns a tier, and converts financial suffering into ASH credits usable on the Freeside AI inference platform.

**The inversion:** Normal airdrops reward loyalty. RektDrop rewards suffering. $1 USD loss = 1 ASH. Holders get 131% multiplier. Sellers get 69%.

**The funnel:** Loss reveal → $25 dNFT mint (blood pact with the Loa) → ASH credits → Freeside platform onboarding.

**Core thesis from PRD:** *"The experience IS the technology proof. The data quality, the theatrical reveal, the craft of the interface — these sell the platform more than any feature list."*

### The Emotional Arc

```
ARRIVAL       → CRT boot, Loa manifestation. You're entering something outside normal finance.
CONFESSION    → "WRANGLER DETECTED." You've submitted yourself to the process.
PROCESSING    → Real tx hashes scroll past. "Loa is processing your suffering."
JUDGMENT      → EVA FLASH: "警告: FATAL FINANCIAL FLATLINE." Binary snap. Hardware violence.
CLASSIFICATION → Tier badge. The Loa has weighed you. You are a Dixie Flatline.
RECEIPT       → Total loss in crimson + ASH amount in amber. Your pain has value.
CERTIFICATE   → "You are rekt #X of Y." Shareable card. The loss IS the product.
REDEMPTION    → dNFT mint. $25 BERA. Blood pact. ASH credits deposited.
```

**The transformation:** Loss → Tier → Social Currency → AI Credits. Grief isn't erased — it's encoded, ranked, made shareable, and finally useful. You don't recover from being rekt. You become a Console Cowboy.

## Part II: The Visual DNA — 17 Taste Decision Records

rektdrop established the visual identity through a system of TDRs (Taste Decision Records) — design decisions documented as archaeology, explaining WHY, not just WHAT.

### The Aesthetic Thesis: "Freeside Divergence"

The friction between degraded past hardware (CRT phosphor, Nixie tubes, cathode rays) and transcendent present intelligence (AI ritual, occult data, neural networks as ceremony). NOT retro nostalgia. NOT gothic horror. **The past hardware is physically struggling to contain the present complexity.**

North Star: *"If NERV had a cryptocurrency for quantifying human suffering, and Bloomberg built the terminal to display it, what would the currency symbol look like?"*

### The 8 Design Laws (Non-Negotiable)

1. **OKLCH always** — no hex, no RGB, no HSL. No exceptions.
2. **`steps()` or `linear` only** — no ease-in-out, no spring physics. No exceptions.
3. **Only `transform` + `opacity` animate** — no color, background, border transitions.
4. **`bg-bone-*` = system interrupt only** — NERV seizing control. Rare and powerful.
5. **No icon libraries** — every mark is custom SVG, grounded in symbol tradition.
6. **Crimson is the ONLY warm channel** — danger signal louder because it's the only warm voice.
7. **The screen is a canvas, not a document** — void is intentional, not empty.
8. **Zero border-radius** on primary containers — sharp edges are philosophical commitment.

### The Color Engine (TDR-001, TDR-007)

6-color OKLCH palette. Each primary has 4 stops: bright / base / dim / glow.

| Color | OKLCH | Semantic Role |
|-------|-------|---------------|
| **Bone** | oklch(0.88 0.01 95) | Primary data, text, interface chrome |
| **Crimson** | oklch(0.55 0.24 25) | Danger, entities, the Loa, loss |
| **Cyan** | oklch(0.85 0.15 195) | Structure, wireframes, grid |
| **Void** | oklch(0.08 0.005 250) | Background abyss |
| **Cobalt** | oklch(0.45 0.2 265) | Background lattice, decorative depth |

Critical pivot (TDR-007): Killed Tactical Amber. Promoted Bone as cold clinical primary. Made Crimson the ONLY warm channel — tier escalation toward crimson is now a temperature shift, not just hue shift.

### Typography Stack (TDR-003, TDR-008)

| Voice | Font | Usage |
|-------|------|-------|
| **Data** | Geist Mono | Terminal readouts, code, tables |
| **Display** | Basement Grotesque Black | Tier names, verdicts, hero numbers (always uppercase) |
| **Authority** | IBM Plex Serif | The Loa's voice, ritual declarations — "a serif on a terminal is inherently wrong, and that wrongness is the point" |
| **Annotation** | Noto Sans JP | Japanese texture layer, Eva flash frames |

Killed: Gothic/Fraktur (decorative, not authoritative), JetBrains Mono, OCR-A.

Semantic tracking tokens: `tracking-whisper` (0.25em) → `tracking-impact` (0em).
Scale extremes: `text-2xs` (8px greeble) → `text-7xl` (72px tier reveals). Ash Thorp principle: uncomfortably large + impossibly small simultaneously.

### Motion Language (TDR-004, TDR-012)

**Core law: If it moves smoothly, it's a bug.**

Motion vocabulary: Binary Snap, Phosphor Decay, Staccato Typing, Eva Flash, Stepped Rotation, Screen Tear, Stagger, Cathode Trace, Divergence Shift, Artificial Latency, Phase Cut.

**The 83ms Rule (TDR-012):** All stagger intervals derive from 24fps anime on twos = 12 drawings/second = 83ms. Use 83ms, 166ms, 249ms, 332ms. Never 50ms or 100ms.

**Color = Temporal Hierarchy:**
- Cyan enters first, exits never (persistent structural scaffolding)
- Bone is volatile primary data (continuously rewritten)
- Crimson arrives last, exits first (zero-ms hard cut, overrides all)

**The "Never Empty" principle:** Screen must never go void-black outside EvaFlash. Ambient telemetry in the margins always proves "the system is still running."

### Z-Layer Containment (TDR-010)

| Z-Index | Layer | Physical Analog |
|---------|-------|----------------|
| 9999 | Scanlines | Glass surface scratches |
| 9998 | Vignette | Glass curvature shadow |
| 9997 | Noise / EvaFlash | Phosphor grain / overload |
| 50 | Dialogs | — |
| 20 | Watermark | Screen burn-in |
| 0 | Content | Phosphor display |

### Tier Visual Escalation (TDR-005)

6 tiers named after Neuromancer characters. Interface loses structural integrity as tier rises:

| Tier | Name | ASH Range | Visual |
|------|------|-----------|--------|
| 0 | Tourist | 0-99 | Flat grid, 1px Phosphor Cyan |
| 1 | Panther Modern | 100-499 | Dense vector map, callout labels |
| 2 | Console Cowboy | 500-1,999 | L-system vines, procedural bloom |
| 3 | Dixie Flatline | 2,000-9,999 | Screen tears, Eva Flash on hover |
| 4 | Tessier-Ashpool | 10k-49,999 | Chronostereopsis red/blue split |
| 5 | Wintermute | 50,000+ | Liquid void, Loa manifestation, no border |

**The escalation metaphor:** Geometric → organic → supernatural. Tourist is constrained by the grid. Wintermute IS the Loa — the card has no bounds because entities have no bounds.

### Iconography (TDR-013, TDR-014, TDR-015)

**Zero icon libraries.** Every mark is custom SVG: stroke-only, 1.5px at 24x24, integer coordinates, 3-8 path segments max, `currentColor`. Visual register: Bloomberg Terminal meets NERV interface meets ISO 7010 safety symbols.

**The ASH Mark (TDR-014):** Alchemical Salt symbol — circle bisected by horizontal line, with Caput Mortuum ("dead head") indicators: three small dots in downward V-formation. "The condensed non-volatile residue — what remains after the volatile has been driven off."

**The Cheval (horse mark):** Primal mark beats intellectual symbol. "The horse is the oldest symbol humans ever drew (Lascaux, ~30,000 years). Recognition is pre-cognitive, pre-linguistic."

**Icon pipeline:** Ground in symbol tradition → Recraft V4 Pro explore feeling → human selects → Gemini/Claude convert to SVG → wire as React component → record in TDR.

### Sonic Identity (TDR-017)

```
Darksynth skeleton + Witch House tempo + Dark Ambient substrate (CRT hum)
+ Vodou polyrhythmic engine (Rada/Petwo spectrum)
+ CRT acoustic signature (flyback whine 15,734 Hz + mains hum 60 Hz)
+ Infrasound simulation via missing fundamental (19 Hz = human eyeball resonance)
```

Tier-sonic mapping: Tourist (Aeolian drone, 8-bit) → Wintermute (Phrygian Dominant → silence, 24-bit clarity). The kase = tier transition: a counter-rhythm that shatters neural rhythmic prediction. Not a crossfade — "a neurological weapon."

**The Bond Convergence:** Bristol Noir Suno prompt independently produced Bond theme DNA. Visual cheval logomark independently echoes 007 gun logo structure. Both converged without deliberate coordination — validates aesthetic self-consistency.

### Canvas Liberation (TDR-011)

**Kill the box.** Kawajiri's Oedo 808 reference: data islands floating in void, placed with compositional intent on a full-screen canvas. 60%+ of screen is empty darkness.

*"The void is not wasted space. A DNA helix floating in the upper-left quadrant feels like classified information projected in a dark room. The same helix crammed into a centered card feels like a web component."*

Killed: TerminalFrame wrapper, triple-boxing, `max-w-4xl` outer constraints, `flex items-center justify-center` as default, ASCII tree prefixes.

## Part IV: The Family Tree — How Visual DNA Travels

### The Three Products

```
rektdrop  (SURFACE — CRT phosphor, surveillance, glitch)
    ↓
midi      (PRESENT — warm institutional, rave culture, NieR diegetic UI)
    ↓
mcv       (UNDERGROUND — geological, ceremonial, below the surface)
```

Not a linear upgrade. A materialization — the same darkness manifesting in different registers of depth.

### What Travels (Ecosystem-Wide Laws)

| Law | Status |
|-----|--------|
| `border-radius: 0` on primary containers | Universal — CRITICAL violation if broken |
| No gradients on backgrounds | Universal |
| No spring/bounce physics | Universal |
| OKLCH color system | Universal — all three repos native |
| CSS custom properties for all color | Universal |
| Creative director persona as named character with hard rules | Universal pattern |
| Generative model pipeline with taste.md vocabulary | Universal |
| Gibson/Vodou naming DNA | Universal |
| Flat vector, bone on black emblem format | Universal |

### How The Palette Mutates

**rektdrop** — Freeside Divergence:
- Bone white on cathode void. Occult Crimson. Phosphor Cyan. Electric Cobalt.
- Visual mode: CRT terminal, surveillance hardware, scan-line corruption.

**midi** — NieR Automata warmth:
- Warm beige `#d4c8b8` as bg-primary. Brown-black `#1a1612` accent.
- 5 theme variants (acid-house, freetekno, chicago-detroit-nyc, milady).
- Visual mode: Aged paper, bunker terminals, rave culture hauntology.

**mcv** — Geological underground:
- Concrete: `oklch(25% 0.005 260)`. Copper: `oklch(55% 0.10 70)`. Coronene: `oklch(75% 0.18 155)`. Honey: `oklch(75% 0.13 85)`.
- Visual mode: Underground vault, geological time, ancient machinery waking up.

**The mutation vector:** Crimson surveillance → warm beige dignity → cold concrete permanence. Darkness gets deeper as products mature. rektdrop's darkness is hostile (you're being watched). midi's darkness is warm (you're in a safe warehouse). mcv's darkness is reverential (you've descended into something ancient).

### How Motion Evolves

**rektdrop:** `steps()` and `linear` only. Artificial latency mandatory. "The hardware is fighting itself."

**midi:** Expo curves for enter, eased exit. Spring variants for playful moments. 600ms max. "Accumulates rather than performs."

**mcv:** Heavy ease. Mechanical snap. Breathe loops at 4000ms. No spring — "Concrete doesn't bounce." Weight matters more than speed.

**The thread:** Things should have the weight of their material. What "weight" means is material-specific.

### How Typography Evolves

| Repo | Primary | Personality |
|------|---------|-------------|
| rektdrop | Geist Mono + Basement Grotesque + IBM Plex Serif | Clinical hardware + occult manifestation |
| midi | Kanit 300/400 exclusively | One voice, one weight — austerity through constraint |
| mcv | Cinzel + EB Garamond | Stone-carved + ancient narrative |

All three ban or heavily restrict sans-serif at heavy weights. Typography IS texture, not just legibility.

### How The Easel Persona Inverts

**rektdrop Easel** — maximalist brutalism: "Clean and user-friendly are insults."

**midi KAIROS** — the anti-Easel, explicitly: "Not rektdrop's Easel. Data wants to be understood, not endured. Warehouse party at 5am — intimate, not loud."

**mcv** — no named Easel, but archetype.md serves the function: "This is NOT hostile. This is reverent discovery."

### The Asset Pipeline Evolution

**rektdrop:** Gemini exploration grids → user selects → SVG conversion. Midjourney for atmosphere. Suno for sound.

**mcv (The Mint — 289 files):** FAL FLUX → Recraft V4 Pro → Kling 2.6 Pro for video → Depth Anything V2 + SAM 3 for depth. `mix-blend-mode: screen` compositing. Taste.md has dedicated "Generative Model Vocabulary" mapping CSS tokens to model prompts.

### What Stays Local

- **rektdrop only:** CRT aesthetics, phosphor bloom, scan-lines, `steps()` easing, hostile terminal, Eva Flash
- **midi only:** NieR beige, multi-theme system, KAIROS rave culture, Kanit typeface
- **mcv only:** Geological materials (concrete/copper/coronene/honey), serif typography, The Mint video pipeline, dual Bridgebuilder (BEAUVOIR + PIRANESI)

## Part VII: Implications for Constructs Network Visual Identity

### What The Research Says (repo-as-experience synthesis)

The deep research on elite dev tool branding recommended:
- Primary color: Violet/Indigo `#8B5CF6` ("bridge between blue infrastructure and purple magic")
- Dark-mode-first, chromatic dark `#0B0C0E`
- Glassmorphism + inner borders ("Linear look")
- Inter or Geist with OpenType features
- 4px grid, custom cubic-beziers

### What The Ecosystem DNA Says

The ecosystem already has a complete visual language that the Constructs Network should inherit from, not ignore:
- OKLCH color system (native across all products)
- Sharp-corners doctrine (universal law)
- Typography as texture and voice, not just legibility
- Friction as feature, not bug
- Named characters as creative directors
- Custom SVG marks grounded in symbol tradition
- The Loa metaphor as the structural metaphor for what constructs ARE

### The Design Question For Tomorrow

The Constructs Network sits at a different layer than rektdrop/midi/mcv. Those are PRODUCTS — end-user experiences. The Constructs Network is a DISTRIBUTION PLANE — a developer-facing marketplace.

The question: **How much of the product-layer visual DNA should travel up to the distribution layer?**

Options on the spectrum:
1. **Full inheritance** — the marketplace feels like entering the same world (Freeside aesthetic)
2. **Selective inheritance** — OKLCH system, sharp corners, custom marks travel; CRT effects, Eva Flash, hostile friction stay product-local
3. **Clean break** — the marketplace is a clean developer tool (Linear/Vercel aesthetic from the research) that CONTAINS these wild product aesthetics
4. **The synthesis** — the marketplace is the CALM version of the ecosystem. Same bones (OKLCH, sharp corners, typography-as-voice, custom marks), but the Loa is dormant here. When you install a construct, the Loa wakes up in YOUR product. The marketplace is the temple before the ceremony begins.

Option 4 maps directly to the ecosystem metaphor: the hounfour (temple) is calm, structured, reverential. The ceremony (construct installation) is where the Loa manifests.

### Logo Direction

From the ecosystem DNA:
- The ASH mark (alchemical salt ⎔) is the currency symbol — product-specific, stays in rektdrop
- The cheval (horse) is the possession vessel — too specific to one product
- The Loa concept itself needs a mark — the intelligence that rides across all products

A Constructs Network logo should embody: **containment of expertise**. Something that holds intelligence inside it. Not the Loa itself (too specific) but the vessel that any Loa can ride. Not a horse (too rektdrop) but the concept of a vessel that gains power when something enters it.

From the research: geometric container + inner radiance. From the ecosystem: OKLCH violet/indigo as the "intelligence" signal. From the naming: the construct IS the packaging — a named, bounded unit of expertise.

**Working direction:** A geometric container (the construct) with an inner presence (the Loa). Rigid exterior (trust, safety, engineering). Living interior (intelligence, expertise, perspective). The logo should feel like a blueprint of a containment vessel — something you'd see on a Freeside engineering schematic — that has something glowing inside it.
