The shapes are the system; the combinations are the individuals.\n\n**Key case study — OVO Energy**: The OVO square serves as the visual DNA reference point. Three motion behaviors (spinning, swiping, scaling) bring it to life. The logo DNA informed iconography, illustrations, and spatial design.\n\n**Dynamic identities principle**: \"Instead of letting the ingredients be the core of the identity, one could also let the system be the constant — whether it is a grid or a set of rules, it forms a language, a formula, that brings everything together.\"\n\n### The Generic Professional Pipeline\n\n| Step | Action | Output |\n|------|--------|--------|\n| 1 | Client Discovery & Brief | Brand understanding |\n| 2 | Application Discovery | Where the logo lives |\n| 3 | Research & Moodboarding | Visual direction |\n| 4 | Hand Sketching (pen on paper) | Raw concepts |\n| 5 | Digitize & Vectorize (Illustrator) | Scalable marks |\n| 6 | Typography & Type Pairing | Font selection/creation |\n| 7 | Grid Construction & Refinement | Precise geometry |\n| 8 | System Rules & Guidelines | Brand bible |\n| 9 | Export & Delivery | Multi-format assets |\n\nKey practitioner insight: sketching by hand is non-negotiable. \"When you're away from the screen, you have no other distractions and your brain has the freedom to wander and explore ideas.\" Digitizing comes after — either via Image Trace (organic/textural sketches) or manual Pen Tool tracing (refined/modern).\n\n---\n\n## 3. SVG Logomark Production Specifications\n\n### ViewBox Conventions\n- **Icon systems**: `viewBox=\"0 0 24 24\"` (Material Design standard) or `viewBox=\"0 0 48 48\"` (Phosphor Icons)\n- **Logo marks**: Match exact bounding box (e.g., `viewBox=\"0 0 340 80\"` for horizontal)\n- **Complex illustrations**: Larger values (e.g., `viewBox=\"0 0 1000 800\"`) for more coordinate precision without fractional values\n- In Illustrator, artboard size directly maps to SVG viewBox\n\n### Grid Systems\n\n**Apple's Icon Grid** (Jony Ive, WWDC 2013):\n- Squircle outer shape (superellipse/Lame curve), three concentric circles, square grid, diagonal lines\n- Derived from golden ratio proportions and inscribed shapes\n- No transparency allowed — all icons funneled into squircle canvas\n- Purpose: every app icon feels like it belongs on the same Home Screen\n\n**Google Material Design Keyline Shapes**:\n- Keyline shapes: circle, square, rectangle, orthogonals, diagonals\n- System icons: 24dp canvas, scalable to 20dp for dense desktop layouts\n- Stroke weight: consistent 2dp width for all instances\n- Corner radius: 2dp on silhouette form, square interior corners\n- Live area (safe zone) keeps content from being cropped\n- Layout grid: 8dp baseline, type on 4dp baseline, toolbar icons on 4dp\n\n**Helena Zhang's Phosphor Icons** (concrete production specs):\n- 48x48px canvas\n- 1.5px centered stroke\n- Rounded end caps\n- Contiguous strokes\n- Straight segments and 15-degree angle increments\n- Keylines: 28x28px circles, 25x25px squares, 28x22px landscape rectangles, 22x28px portrait rectangles\n- 6px thick trim area\n\n### Stroke Width Systems\n- Default industry standard: **2px**\n- Common alternative: **1.5px**\n- Minimum: **1px** (below this, illegible)\n- Use whole-pixel or half-pixel increments only (avoid 1.215px — causes blur)\n- **Center-aligned strokes** (not inside or outside) — CSS stroke renders as center\n- Export as SVG with strokes intact — do NOT outline/expand strokes\n\n### Production Best Practices\n- Output icons in monochrome (#000000 or #FFFFFF), colorize via CSS\n- Always include both `viewBox` and `width`/`height` attributes\n- Merge paths and minimize layers\n- Pixel-snap to grid — Carbon Design System requires no decimals in coordinates\n- `aria-hidden=\"true\"` for decorative SVGs\n- Scale at multiples of the grid (24px, 48px, 96px)\n\n### The Four Logo Grid Types (per Akrivi)\n1. **Base Grid** — Initial geometric structure\n2. **Construction Grid** — Refine curves, alignment, spacing after design\n3. **Lockup Grid** — Align mark + wordmark with optical balance\n4. **Clearspace Grid** — Define padding/safe space\n\n---\n\n## 4. Mark + Type Lockup Rules\n\n### Spacing Ratios\n- **Mark-to-wordmark separation**: Use 1/3 of logotype height as clearance between mark and wordmark\n- **Wordmark width alignment**: Duplicate the logomark left and right (edge-to-edge, no space), then scale logotype to fit between outer ends. This aligns text width optically with the mark.\n\n### Clear Space (the \"X\" unit)\n- Define a unit \"x\" derived from a visible element in the logo (letter height, mark dimension)\n- Google uses the \"G\" height; PayPal uses the \"P\" height\n- Apply \"x\" as minimum padding on all sides\n- Choose whole numbers or easy ratios — avoid making people do complex math\n- Same \"x\" unit throughout the entire brand guideline\n\n### Minimum Size Requirements\n| Context | Minimum |\n|---------|---------|\n| Print (horizontal) | 1.5 inches wide |\n| Print (vertical) | 1.25 inches wide |\n| Digital (primary) | 60-80px height |\n| Digital (smallest) | 20px |\n| Favicon | 16x16px |\n\n### Lockup Types\n- **Horizontal**: Mark beside wordmark (primary, most common)\n- **Vertical**: Mark above wordmark (square contexts, social avatars)\n- **Mark-only**: When space is too small for full lockup (favicons, app icons)\n\n### Integrity Rules\n- Never separate, rearrange, or resize components independently\n- Never alter shape, color, or proportions outside approved guidelines\n- No outlines, drop shadows, gradients, or glow effects unless explicitly approved\n- Dark backgrounds use inverted (white) lockup\n- WCAG contrast: minimum 3:1 for logos, 4.5:1 for text\n\n---\n\n## 5.
