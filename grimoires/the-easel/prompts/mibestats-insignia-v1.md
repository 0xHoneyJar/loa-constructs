**The cathedral-bazaar split**: "If Milady is the cathedral, Mibera is the bazaar in the square outside." MibeStats is the bazaar's intelligence service — the vendor who knows every transaction, every wallet, every flow.

## Typography Register: MONOSPACE TECHNICAL × BLACKLETTER HYBRID

**Why this hybrid**: No existing register captures MibeStats. The site already lives at the intersection of medieval codex (Pirata One blackletter headings) and hacker terminal (Share Tech Mono monospace body). This tension IS the identity — ancient knowledge systems rendered through machine interfaces. The blackletter says "this knowledge has been here forever." The monospace says "and now a machine reads it."

**Fonts to explore**:
- **Share Tech Mono** (Google Fonts) — the site's native body font, terminal authenticity
- **Fira Code** (free) — ligatures, ink traps, coder aesthetic
- **JetBrains Mono** (free) — sharper, more angular, dev-tool energy
- **Pirata One** (Google Fonts) — the site's native heading font, blackletter/gothic

**Type treatment**:
- All caps or mixed case, medium tracking (0.1-0.15em)
- The "Stats" portion could shift register — "MIBE" in blackletter energy, "STATS" in monospace
- Weight: regular — the mark carries visual weight, type stays technical
- Consider: the word "MIBE" is already a truncation (from Mibera) — it has the energy of a callsign, a handle, an alias

## Pairing Rules

- Mark stroke weight at 160px: **1.25-1.5px** (matching monospace font optical weight)
- The mark is the HEAVY element; the type is the TECHNICAL element. Density vs precision.
- Consider dual-register treatment: "MIBE" in one weight/style, "STATS" in another
- At mark-only scale (<48px): just the mark. At card scale (48-200px): horizontal lockup. At hero scale (>200px): stacked lockup with mark above.
- The mark should feel like it belongs on a darknet terminal — something you'd see booting up on a CRT, not something designed in Figma
