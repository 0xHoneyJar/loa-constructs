# Beehive — Single-Focus Logo Prompts v2

> **Construct**: Beehive (slug: observer) — "The Eye That Watches"
> **Identity reframe**: NOT surveillance. Observation + gentle probing. Like a naturalist who watches a deer in a clearing, then asks the forest the right question. Patient, calm, present — but not passive. Asks the questions that reveal what people actually mean (Mom Test style). Gecko on a warm wall — sees everything, requires almost nothing, and when it speaks, the question cuts to truth.
> **Pipeline**: Recraft V4 Pro (`recraft/v4/pro/text-vector`) on Fal.ai
> **Style**: `vector_illustration/sharp_contrast`
> **Color**: `[{"r": 245, "g": 240, "b": 232}]`
